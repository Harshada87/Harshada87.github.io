Delete your FocusCoach account and data

FocusCoach is published on Google Play by HM Craft Labs (Harshada Mahulkar).

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open FocusCoach and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately. If the app asks you to sign in again first, that's a security check.

Option 2: by email (no app needed)

Email harshada.mahulkar@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my FocusCoach account", sent from the email address you signed up with. We'll confirm and delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, name and account ID, stored with Google Firebase Authentication.

What stays, and how to remove it

  • Focus history, goals and distraction list are stored only on your phone, never on our servers. Remove them by clearing the app's storage (Android Settings › Apps › FocusCoach › Storage › Clear storage) or by uninstalling.
  • Crash reports aren't linked to your account and are deleted automatically by Firebase Crashlytics within 90 days.
  • Premium purchase records are kept by Google Play under its own policy. Cancel a subscription in the Play Store › Payments & subscriptions.
