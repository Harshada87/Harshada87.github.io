FocusCoach privacy policy

Effective 16 November 2026

FocusCoach (Android package com.my.focuscoach) is made by Harshada Mahulkar, publishing as HM Craft Labs ("we"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and the choices you have. We don't sell your data.

Summary

  • Your focus sessions, history, goals and distraction list stay on your phone.
  • An account is optional. If you create one, we store your email address and name with Google Firebase.
  • When you drift during a session, a short, anonymous description of the moment is sent to Google's Gemini AI to write one reflective question.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Premium has no ads.
  • Crash reports help us fix bugs.

Service providers

We rely on Google services, each with its own privacy policy: Firebase (Authentication, AI Logic, Crashlytics, App Check), AdMob, and Google Play. Data may be processed in the United States and other countries where Google operates.

Your choices

  • Usage Access is off until you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time in Android settings.
  • Ads: in regions that require it, you're asked for consent; change it anytime in Settings › Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Premium removes ads.
  • Notifications can be turned off in Android settings.
  • Use without an account: everything except sign-in works without one.

Keeping and deleting data

  • On-device data stays until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • Your account is kept until you delete it. Delete it in the app (Profile › Delete account) or request deletion here. We delete it within 30 days.
  • Crash reports are kept by Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
  • Gemini requests aren't stored by us.

Security

Data sent to Google services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Firebase App Check helps make sure requests come from the genuine app.

Children

FocusCoach isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your data, or to object to its use. Email us and we'll respond within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
